HYPERLOCALMART — REFUND, CANCELLATION, WALLET AND MEMBERSHIP POLICY

This policy is part of the Terms. It states how money, store credit, and free-delivery packs work on HyperLocalMart.

1. YOU CANNOT CANCEL AFTER PLACE
The buyer app does not offer “cancel order”. If something cannot be packed, the vendor (or hub, where allowed) rejects or cancels those lines.

2. SHOP REJECT / LINE CANCEL
• That shop’s rejected value is not payable to the vendor.
• Other shops on the same order may still deliver.
• You receive store credit for cancelled lines as posted in Wallet (COD and remaining multi-shop cases). If the whole order is cancelled and you paid online, we unwind via the payment gateway refund where that path applies (typically up to 5 working days after the provider processes it), instead of duplicating the same rupees as wallet.
• If the last remaining shop is rejected, membership free-delivery credit used on that order is restored.

3. RESTORE BEFORE PICKUP
A shop may restore a cancelled line only if your wallet still holds the store credit issued for that line. If you already spent it at a later checkout, restore is refused (“already used this store credit”).

4. WALLET (STORE CREDIT)
• Not withdrawable to bank/UPI.
• Optional at next checkout: tick “Use store credit” or leave it.
• Also credited for approved claims and revealed scratch cards.
• No expiry is coded on wallet rupees today; Super Admin may change product rules later via these policies.
• Fraud or abuse may freeze credit.

5. CLAIMS (7 DAYS AFTER DELIVERY)
File only on delivered, non-cancelled items, within 7 days: wrong, missing, or damaged. Hub may approve store credit (charged to vendor payout) or reject. Duplicate or out-of-window claims are refused.

6. MEMBERSHIP / FREE DELIVERY PACKS
• Prepaid credits, not auto-renew.
• Used only when a delivery fee would have been charged.
• Expire at pack end; leftover credits become 0.
• New pack adds credits and resets expiry from activation for that pack length.
• Cash-at-hub packs activate only when hub confirms cash.
• No refund of pack price after activation, except where law requires. Unused deliveries are not paid out in cash.
• Credit restored if we cancel / fail delivery after a consume.

7. SCRATCH CARDS
Promotional wallet credit. Not guaranteed on every order. Town Super Admin settings control on/off, min goods amount, and reward range.

8. COD
You pay remaining cash to the agent. Hub close-day reconciles agent cash. A COD “refund” is store credit and/or a lower cash-due, not a bank transfer.

9. ONLINE PAY
When enabled, failed pay means the order is not placed. Successful pay that is later fully unwound follows the gateway refund timeline (about 5 working days). Partial shop reject on a still-active order uses store credit as above.

10. HOW TO ASK
Use in-app Wallet and Order detail first. Then the hub / support phone on the login screen. Keep the order number handy.

Super Admin may update this text at any time. The app always shows the current version.
