KOYAKART — PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains how KoyaKart uses personal data on the buyer, vendor, hub/agent, and super-admin portals. It should be read with the Terms.

1. DATA WE COLLECT
• Account: mobile number, name, password hash, role (buyer / vendor / hub / agent / super-admin), town.
• Buyer: saved addresses, cart and orders, payment method (COD / online marker), store-credit ledger, membership packs and credits, claims, scratch cards, device/session tokens.
• Vendor: shop profile, listings, order bags, payouts, commercial terms, optional ID or bank details Super Admin stores for settlement.
• Hub / agent: assignment history, COD close-day entries, OTP events, membership cash confirmations.
• Support: what you type in claims and reject reasons; call recordings are not in-app unless you call the published support number on your own phone.

We do not ask buyers for Aadhaar or PAN in the current apps.

2. WHY WE USE IT
To run the town marketplace: login, show the right catalog, place and deliver orders, collect COD, apply store credit and free-delivery credits, handle claims, pay vendors, detect abuse, and contact you on the support number you see in Settings.

3. WHO SEES WHAT
• Vendors see order bags for their shop, not your full address book, and not other shops’ payouts.
• Hub and assigned agents see what they need to pick up and deliver (address, phone, OTP flow, COD amount).
• Super Admin sees platform-wide ops, reports, and legal settings.
• Payment providers (when online pay is on) receive what they need to charge or refund. We do not store full card numbers in our databases.
• We do not sell your phone book to advertisers. Catalog ads are platform/vendor promotions inside the app.

4. RETENTION
Orders, invoices, wallet and membership ledgers, and COD close records are kept for tax, dispute, and ops history. You may ask hub/support to correct a name or address. Closing an account does not erase completed order records we must keep by law.

5. SECURITY
Passwords are hashed. OTPs and hub PINs are operational secrets — do not share them. No method is perfect; tell us quickly if you think your account was used by someone else.

6. YOUR CHOICES
Update addresses in the buyer app. Change password from the portal that issued your login. Withdraw consent by stopping use and asking Super Admin/support to disable the account. Some processing (invoices, COD, claims already filed) must continue.

7. CHILDREN
The Platform is for adults placing grocery orders. Do not register a child under 18 as the account holder.

8. UPDATES
Super Admin may edit this policy in Platform settings. The in-app version and date control.

9. CONTACT & GRIEVANCE (DPDP)
The platform operator (data fiduciary) is named in Super Admin → Settings as supplier legal name. For privacy questions, correction, or complaints, use the support phone and grievance officer shown on login and legal screens. We will acknowledge grievances within a reasonable time and work to resolve them under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 where it applies.

10. LEGAL BASIS (PLAIN LANGUAGE)
• Contract: account, orders, delivery, payments, wallet, membership, and vendor settlement.
• Legitimate interest: fraud prevention, security logs, and improving town operations (balanced against your rights).
• Consent: optional promotional SMS/push where we ask separately in the app; you can turn marketing off in Privacy & data.
• Legal obligation: tax, accounting, and records we must keep after a transaction.

11. MARKETING
We may send order and security messages without a separate marketing opt-in. Promotional offers (deals, referral nudges, scratch-card reminders) use the marketing preference in your account when that channel is enabled. Turning marketing off does not stop transactional messages.

12. COOKIES & DEVICE STORAGE
Web portals store login tokens and preferences (theme, town, cart) in your browser or app storage. We do not use third-party advertising cookies in the default product. If we add analytics or ad pixels later, we will update this policy and the cookie notice before turning them on.

13. THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS
We use service providers that process data on our instructions, including cloud hosting (India region target), payment gateways (Razorpay and/or PhonePe/Cashfree when enabled), SMS for OTP, and push notification delivery (FCM). They receive only what is needed for their service. We do not sell your personal data.

14. YOUR RIGHTS
You may update profile and addresses in the app. You may turn off marketing consent in Privacy & data. You may delete your buyer account there (password required); we anonymize your login profile and remove saved addresses while keeping order and payment records we must retain. For data not available in self-service, contact the grievance officer. Export of a full machine-readable dump may be provided on verified request where feasible.

15. DATA BREACH
If a personal-data breach affects you, we will investigate, mitigate, and notify you and authorities as required by applicable Indian law and the severity of the incident.

16. CONTACT
Use the support phone and grievance officer named in Super Admin → Settings / login screen.
